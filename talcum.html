Frequently Asked Questions

How is talcum powder linked to ovarian cancer?+
Studies have shown that talc particles applied to the genital area can migrate to the ovaries through the reproductive tract and cause inflammation that may lead to cancer. Internal J&J documents revealed the company was aware of this risk for decades while continuing to market the product as safe.
J&J filed for bankruptcy — can I still file a claim?+
J&J's bankruptcy maneuvers have been challenged in courts. Filing a claim now is critical to preserve your place in the compensation process. Your attorney will advise you on the current status of the litigation and your best options.
What if I no longer have the product or receipts?+
You do not need product receipts or containers to file a claim. Medical records establishing your diagnosis and your testimony about product use are typically sufficient. A licensed attorney can guide you through what documentation is needed.
How long do I have to file?+
Most states allow 2–3 years from the date of your cancer diagnosis. Given J&J's ongoing bankruptcy proceedings, acting quickly is especially important. Contact us today for a free consultation.
