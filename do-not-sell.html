Your Right to Opt Out

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and similar state privacy laws, you have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. We extend this right to all US residents, not just California residents.

When you submit a form on our website, your name, email address, phone number, and case details may be shared with licensed attorneys in our network who pay referral fees to receive leads. This constitutes the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under applicable law.

Once your opt-out request is processed, we will stop selling or sharing your personal information for a period of 12 months. You will not be penalized or denied any service for exercising this right.

What Happens After You Opt Out

  • We will stop sharing your personal information with attorney partners within 5 business days of your verified request.
  • Your opt-out will remain in effect for 12 months. After 12 months, we may contact you to ask if you wish to re-opt-in.
  • You will not be discriminated against, denied service, or treated differently for exercising this right.
  • Information already shared prior to your request cannot be recalled from attorneys who received it, but no new sharing will occur.

Additional Privacy Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may also have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Delete: Request that we delete your personal information
  • Correct: Request that we correct inaccurate information we hold about you

To exercise any of these rights, please email privacy@getyourclaimnow.com or review our full Privacy Policy.
