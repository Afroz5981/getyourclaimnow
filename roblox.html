Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I sue Roblox for what happened to my child?+
Yes. Families are pursuing civil lawsuits against Roblox Corporation alleging negligence in failing to protect children from predators and harmful content on the platform. A licensed attorney can evaluate the specific facts of your case during a free consultation.
Does Section 230 protect Roblox from lawsuits?+
Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act provides limited immunity to online platforms but does not protect companies from all liability. Attorneys have successfully argued claims involving negligent design, failure to warn, and direct participation in harm. An attorney can advise whether your specific claim has merit.
How long do I have to file a claim?+
Statutes of limitations vary by state and claim type, typically 2–3 years. For claims involving minors, the clock may not start until the child turns 18 in some states. Contact us today for a free evaluation.
Do I need to have reported it to file a lawsuit?+
No. A police report is not required to pursue a civil claim. Many families choose not to involve law enforcement. Your attorney will advise you on the best path forward based on your specific situation.
