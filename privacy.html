Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last Updated: June 11, 2026

1. Who We Are

Get Your Claim Now ("we," "us," or "our") is a legal referral and lead generation service — not a law firm. We operate the website getyourclaimnow.com and connect individuals who may have legal claims with licensed attorneys in our network.

We do not provide legal advice, legal representation, or legal services of any kind. Submitting a form on our website does not create an attorney-client relationship with us.

Important: Get Your Claim Now is a referral service. Attorneys in our network pay referral fees to receive leads. This does not affect the quality of representation you receive, but we disclose it in the interest of full transparency.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a form on our website, we collect the following information:

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • The type of legal claim you are inquiring about
  • Your IP address (collected automatically)
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent on site, and referring URL
  • Date and time of form submission

We may also collect information you voluntarily provide in any free-text fields, such as descriptions of your situation.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide for the following purposes:

Referral Matching

Your information is used to match you with licensed attorneys in our network who handle your type of legal claim. Your contact details are shared with those attorneys so they can reach out to discuss your case.

Communication

We may use your contact information to follow up regarding your inquiry, confirm your submission was received, or inform you of updates to our services.

Service Improvement

We analyze aggregated, anonymized data to improve the quality of our referral matching, website performance, and user experience.

Legal Compliance

We may use or retain your information as required by law, court order, or regulatory authority, including for fraud prevention and compliance purposes.

4. Who We Share Your Information With

We share your personal information with the following parties:

Attorney Partners

Licensed attorneys and law firms in our network who have agreed to our terms and who handle your type of claim. These attorneys pay referral fees to receive your information. They may contact you by phone, email, or text.

Service Providers

Third-party vendors who assist us in operating our website, storing data, and processing form submissions. These vendors are contractually required to protect your information and may not use it for their own purposes.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal process, such as a court order or subpoena.

We do not sell your information to data brokers, marketers, or any parties unrelated to legal services for your specific claim type.

5. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Know: You may request a copy of the personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to Delete: You may request that we delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: You may request that we correct inaccurate personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Opt Out: You may request that we stop selling or sharing your personal information with attorney partners. Visit our Do Not Sell My Information page to submit a request.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not penalize you or deny you services for exercising any of your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in Section 9 below. We will respond to verified requests within 45 days.

6. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants you specific rights regarding your personal information. In the past 12 months, we have collected the categories of personal information described in Section 2, and shared them with attorney partners as described in Section 4.

You have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. To do so, visit our Do Not Sell My Information page.

We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we will treat it as a valid opt-out request and will not sell or share your information.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including matching you with attorneys and maintaining compliance records. In general, we retain form submission data for up to 24 months. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

8. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to analyze traffic, improve user experience, and measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though doing so may affect the functionality of our website.

We may use third-party analytics tools such as Google Analytics. These tools collect anonymized usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have collected information from a minor, we will promptly delete it. If you believe a minor has submitted information through our site, please contact us immediately.

10. Security

We implement reasonable technical and administrative safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
